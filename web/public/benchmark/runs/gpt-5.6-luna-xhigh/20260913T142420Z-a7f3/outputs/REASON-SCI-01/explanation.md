# REASON-SCI-01 explanation

- **S1 = 5.** The B values sum to `4 + 5 + 6 = 15`; with three seedlings,
  the mean is `15 / 3 = 5 cm`.
- **S2 = B-C.** Both groups receive 8 hours of light, while B has no
  fertilizer and C has fertilizer. Their pair is the comparison that changes
  fertilizer while holding the stated light condition fixed.
- **S3 = A-B.** A and B are both unfertilized, so this pair changes light
  duration (4 versus 8 hours) while holding fertilizer status fixed.
- **S4 = not_established.** The table gives one variety and one experiment;
  the B-C difference in these seedlings cannot establish an exact effect for
  every variety. No significance or generalization claim follows from the
  table alone.
- **S5 = not_established.** A and C differ in both light duration and
  fertilizer, so their mean difference mixes those factors. The B-C contrast
  isolates fertilizer at 8 hours, but it does not make the C-A difference a
  fertilizer-only effect.

An additional experiment should use a randomized factorial design crossing
4/8-hour light with fertilizer absent/present, with replicated seedlings (and,
for the “all varieties” claim, multiple varieties). That supplies both
fertilizer levels at each light duration and permits the interaction to be
examined; it still should not claim an effect without an appropriate
statistical analysis.
